A little preparation goes a long way

Allergens deserve attention.

Milk and dairy

Cocoa Cloud includes milk and ice cream in the sample recipe.

Oats and wheat

Berry Bliss uses oat milk. Avocado Crunch contains wheat and gluten.

Shared equipment

All items may encounter allergens through shared surfaces or equipment. Ask before ordering.

Your blend, your preferences

Ask about the details.

Ingredient swaps can change flavour, texture, allergens and nutrition. Confirm the final recipe with the café before choosing.

Before you choose, ask which ingredients are included in the current recipe and whether any substitutions are available. Milk alternatives, syrups and toppings can change the finished drink, so check the complete ingredient list rather than relying on the menu name alone.

If you have an allergy or a specific dietary requirement, discuss shared equipment and preparation with the café. Verified nutrition details should refer to the final recipe and portion size; the sample menu does not provide confirmed nutrient figures.

  • Check the milk or plant-based alternative
  • Ask about syrups and toppings
  • Confirm preparation and cross-contact practices
