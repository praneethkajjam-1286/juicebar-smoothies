Frequently asked questions.

Can I request a substitution?

Use the contact form to ask about ingredient swaps. Availability and final recipes must be confirmed by the café.

Are the displayed prices and offers final?

No. This website is a template with illustrative products, prices and promotions. Final availability, pricing and terms must be confirmed before launch.

Where can I find allergen information?

Every product detail page lists sample ingredients and allergen notes. The café must verify final recipes and preparation practices before serving.
