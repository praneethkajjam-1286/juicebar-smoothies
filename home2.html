Before you blend

A few helpful answers.

What is the difference between juice and a smoothie?

Juice is extracted from fruit or vegetables. Smoothies blend ingredients together and usually retain more of their pulp and texture.

Can I request a substitution?

Use the contact form to ask about ingredient swaps. Availability and final recipes must be confirmed by the café.

Do you offer dairy-free choices?

The sample menu includes fruit juices and blends using oat or coconut milk. Check each item’s ingredients and ask about cross-contact.
